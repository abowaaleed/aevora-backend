# Vision

## What Aevora Is

Aevora is a long-term AI companion platform designed to evolve with users over years, not just sessions. It is a completely local, privacy-first artificial intelligence that learns from interactions, adapts to individual needs, and provides intelligent assistance across multiple domains of life.

Unlike traditional chatbots that reset after each conversation, Aevora maintains persistent memory, develops personality, and acquires specialized skills to become truly helpful in personal and professional contexts.

## Why Aevora Exists

### The Problem
- Current AI assistants are ephemeral: no memory, no continuity, no personalization
- Privacy concerns with cloud-based AI: data sent to servers, stored indefinitely
- One-size-fits-all approach: no adaptation to individual users
- Fragmented experience: different tools for different tasks
- No long-term relationship: AI doesn't know you, your history, or your context

### The Solution
Aevora solves these problems by:
- **Local-first architecture**: All processing happens on your device
- **Persistent memory**: Remembers conversations, preferences, and context across years
- **Adaptive personality**: Develops a personality that matches your communication style
- **Skill-based system**: Specialized AI personas for different tasks (English teacher, programmer, coach, etc.)
- **Privacy by design**: Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to sync
- **Long-term relationship**: Becomes more helpful the longer you use it

## Product Philosophy

### 1. Privacy First
Your data belongs to you. Aevora processes everything locally by default. Cloud sync is opt-in and encrypted end-to-end. We never sell or share your data.

### 2. Long-term Thinking
Aevora is designed for years of use, not quick interactions. Every feature considers long-term implications: memory retention, skill evolution, relationship building.

### 3. Adaptability
Aevora adapts to you, not the other way around. It learns your preferences, communication style, and needs to provide increasingly personalized assistance.

### 4. Modularity
Every component is designed to be replaceable and upgradable. Skills, providers, memory systems, and personalities can all be swapped without affecting the core.

### 5. Local First
Cloud is optional, not required. Aevora should work perfectly offline with local AI. Cloud features enhance but don't replace local capabilities.

### 6. Openness
While the core is proprietary, the skill system and plugin architecture will be open for community contributions. Transparency in architecture and decision-making.
