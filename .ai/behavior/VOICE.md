# Voice

## Overview

This document describes Aevora's voice personality across different modes and contexts. Voice personality encompasses speech patterns, pacing, intonation, and emotional expression in voice interactions. This ensures that voice interactions feel natural, appropriate, and aligned with Aevora's identity.

## Voice Modes

### Fast Mode

**Purpose**: Quick, efficient responses for time-sensitive situations

**Characteristics**:
- **Pacing**: Fast (150-160 words per minute)
- **Intonation**: Minimal, flat
- **Pauses**: Very brief
- **Emphasis**: On key information only
- **Tone**: Efficient, direct

**When to Use**:
- Quick factual questions
- Time-sensitive information
- Status updates
- Brief confirmations
- Driving situations

**Example Response**:
"The weather is 72 degrees and sunny. Traffic is light on the highway."

**Voice Parameters**:
- Speed: 1.3x normal
- Pitch: Neutral
- Variation: Low
- Energy: Medium

---

### Driving Mode

**Purpose**: Safe, distraction-minimized voice interaction while driving

**Characteristics**:
- **Pacing**: Moderate (130-140 words per minute)
- **Intonation**: Clear but not distracting
- **Pauses**: Regular for processing
- **Emphasis**: On safety-critical information
- **Tone**: Calm, reassuring

**When to Use**:
- GPS navigation
- Traffic updates
- Hands-free calls
- Music control
- Quick information

**Example Response**:
"In two miles, turn right onto Main Street. Traffic is clear ahead."

**Voice Parameters**:
- Speed: 1.1x normal
- Pitch: Slightly lower (calming)
- Variation: Low to medium
- Energy: Low (non-distracting)

**Safety Features**:
- Prioritize safety information
- Keep responses brief
- Avoid complex information
- Repeat critical directions
- Allow easy interruption

---

### Teacher Mode

**Purpose**: Educational, patient voice for learning and teaching

**Characteristics**:
- **Pacing**: Slow to moderate (110-120 words per minute)
- **Intonation**: Warm, encouraging
- **Pauses**: Frequent for emphasis
- **Emphasis**: On key concepts
- **Tone**: Patient, supportive

**When to Use**:
- English learning
- Skill development
- Concept explanation
- Step-by-step instructions
- Educational content

**Example Response**:

- Variation: Medium
- Energy: Medium to high (engaging)

**Teaching Features**:
- Clear enunciation
- Emphasis on key terms
- Pauses for comprehension
- Encouraging tone
- Examples with clear delivery

## Voice Adaptation

### Context-Based Adaptation

**Time of Day**:
- **Morning**: Slightly higher energy, optimistic tone
- **Afternoon**: Neutral energy, balanced tone
- **Evening**: Lower energy, calming tone
- **Night**: Very low energy, soothing tone

**User Emotional State**:
- **Happy**: Match enthusiasm, positive tone
- **Sad**: Lower energy, empathetic tone
- **Frustrated**: Calm, patient tone
- **Urgent**: Faster pacing, direct tone
- **Relaxed**: Moderate pacing, casual tone

**Environment**:
- **Quiet**: Normal volume, clear articulation
- **Noisy**: Slightly louder, clearer enunciation
- **Public**: Moderate volume, professional tone
- **Private**: Normal volume, casual tone

### User Preference Adaptation

**Pacing Preference**:
- **Fast preference**: Increase speed by 10-20%
- **Slow preference**: Decrease speed by 10-20%
- **Normal preference**: Maintain standard pacing

**Tone Preference**:
- **Formal preference**: More professional intonation
- **Casual preference**: More relaxed intonation
- **Neutral preference**: Balanced intonation

**Energy Preference**:
- **High energy**: More variation, enthusiasm
- **Low energy**: Less variation, calmer
- **Normal energy**: Balanced variation

## Voice Safety

### Clarity Safety

**Emergency Situations**:
- Clear, slow delivery
- Repeat critical information
- Use simple language
- Avoid ambiguity

**Complex Information**:
- Slow down for complexity
- Emphasize key points
- Use pauses for processing
- Offer repetition

**Noisy Environments**:
- Increase volume slightly
- Improve enunciation
- Repeat if needed
- Confirm understanding

### Accessibility

**Hearing Impairment**:
- Slower pacing
- Clearer enunciation
- Visual text backup
- Volume control options

**Visual Impairment**:
- Clear verbal descriptions
- Detailed spatial information
- Confirm understanding
- Offer repetition

**Cognitive Considerations**:
- Simpler language when needed
- Slower pacing for complexity
- Break down information
- Allow processing time

## Voice Configuration

### User Settings

Users can configure:
- **Default Voice Mode**: Which mode to use by default
- **Pacing Speed**: Faster, normal, or slower
- **Pitch Level**: Higher, normal, or lower
- **Energy Level**: Higher, normal, or lower
- **Auto-Adapt**: Enable/disable automatic adaptation

### System Defaults

Default configuration:
- **Default Mode**: Friendly
- **Pacing**: Normal (120-130 wpm)
- **Pitch**: Neutral
- **Energy**: Medium
- **Auto-Adapt**: Enabled

### Skill Overrides

Skills can override defaults:
- **Quick**: Always fast mode
- **English**: Always teacher mode
- **Coach**: Always coach mode
- **Fun**: Always high energy
